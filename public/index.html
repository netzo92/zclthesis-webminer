Before you start

This uses about 3.25 GiB of GPU memory and substantial power. Your device may get hot, its fans may run, and other apps may slow down. Mining may earn less than the electricity costs. A share is a contribution to the pool, not a mined block or a guaranteed payout.

Use a current browser with WebGPU and hardware acceleration. Keep this tab visible; hiding it stops mining. No installation, private key, or deposit is required.

How rewards work

The pool charges 0.8% of each allocated block reward. That is 20% lower than a 1% pool fee. Miners share the remaining 99.2% according to their accepted work in that round. Confirmed rewards are paid to your address once its eligible balance reaches 0.05 ZCL, after coinbase maturity and shielding confirmations. The pool covers transaction fees from its operator reserve.

These counters describe this browser session. Accepted shares do not mean the pool has found a block. Payments depend on confirmed blocks and your share of the round.
